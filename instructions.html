FuelSharp

How to use

Find the cheapest fuel near you — at the price you pay

FuelSharp shows live fuel prices from the NSW Government’s FuelCheck data, applies your own discounts (NRMA, shopper dockets, fuel cards…), and can show the cost in XDC or USDC.

Quick start

  1. Pick your fuelTap a fuel type at the top: U91, E10, P95, P98, Diesel, Premium Diesel, LPG or E85.
  2. Find your areaTap the locate button (◎) on the map to jump to where you are, or drag and zoom the map yourself.
  3. Read the mapEach pin shows the price in cents per litre. cheapest third middle dearest third of what’s on screen. When you’re zoomed far out, pins become coloured dots — zoom in to see prices.
  4. Check the listThe list ranks every station in the map area. Tap a row to fly to it; tap a pin for all its prices and a Directions link. Once you’ve used the locate button you can sort by Distance too.
  5. Add your discountsUnder Your discounts, choose a brand, enter the cents-per-litre off, and save. Discounted prices turn green and the ranking updates. Note some chains appear under several brand names (e.g. Ampol, EG Ampol, Ampol Foodary) — add each one you can use.
  6. See your savingsThe summary shows the cheapest price in view, the typical (median) price, and how much you save on a full tank. Set your tank size under Settings.

Paying with crypto?

Switch AUD / XDC / USDC at the top right to see each price converted at the current market rate (shown under Settings). Handy if you use a card that spends XDC or stablecoins.

Install it like an app

On Android/Chrome tap Install app. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari.

Where the data comes from

Prices come from the NSW Government FuelCheck API and are refreshed regularly. The status pill at the top shows when they were last updated, and a warning appears if they’re getting old. Each station also shows when its price was last reported.

Your discounts and settings are stored only in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. Always confirm the price at the pump.

About XDC Network

XDC Network is a fast, low-cost blockchain built for real-world payments. Showing fuel prices in XDC and USDC makes it easy for crypto-card users to see what a fill-up really costs.
