# XDC FuelSharp NSW

**Real-time NSW fuel prices with your personal discounts**

XDC FuelSharp NSW is a simple tool that shows you the cheapest fuel prices near you in New South Wales, based on official government data. You can add your own discounts (like NRMA, work fuel cards, or loyalty programs) and even see what the price would be if you paid in **XDC** or **USDC** (on the XDC Network).

---

## What is it?

XDC FuelSharp NSW helps you find the best fuel prices in New South Wales. It shows live prices from petrol stations and lets you:

- See the cheapest stations near you on a map
- Add your personal discounts so you see your **actual** price
- View prices in **XDC** or **USDC** (useful if you use cards that let you spend crypto)

It’s designed to be simple and useful for everyday drivers.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Open the site**  
   Go to: [https://s4njk4n.github.io/XDC_FuelSharpNSW/](https://s4njk4n.github.io/XDC_FuelSharpNSW/)

2. **Choose your fuel type**  
   Tap U91, E10, P95, P98, Diesel, Premium Diesel, LPG or E85 at the top.

3. **Look at the map**  
   Tap the locate button (◎) to jump to where you are, or drag/zoom yourself. Pins show the price in ¢/L and are coloured green → amber → red from cheapest to dearest on screen. Tap a pin for all of that station's prices and a Directions link.

4. **Check the ranked list**  
   Next to (or below) the map you’ll see every station in the map area ranked by your effective price, plus a summary of the cheapest price, the typical price and what you save on a full tank. After using locate, you can also sort by distance.

5. **Add your discounts** (optional but useful)
   - Scroll down to **"Your Discounts"**
   - Select a brand (e.g. United, Ampol, Shell, etc.) — brands on the map right now are listed first
   - Enter how many cents per litre cheaper you get
   - Add a note (e.g. “NRMA”, “Work card”, “Woolies”)
   - Click **Save discount**

   Once saved, your discounted price will automatically appear in the list.

6. **View prices in XDC or USDC** (optional)
   Use the **AUD / XDC / USDC** switch at the top to change between:
   - AUD (normal dollars)
   - XDC
   - USDC (on the XDC Network)

   This is handy if you use payment cards that let you spend XDC or stablecoins.

7. **Save to your phone** (recommended)
   On mobile, tap the share button and choose **“Add to Home Screen”**. It will work like a normal app.

## How It Works

XDC FuelSharp NSW pulls live fuel price data directly from the **NSW Government’s official Fuel API**. This means the prices you see are the same ones published by the state government.

The tool also lets you see what those prices would cost in **XDC** or **USDC** (a stablecoin on the XDC Network). This is especially useful for people who use crypto payment cards, as it gives them a quick way to compare real-world spending power.

All your discounts are saved privately in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.
