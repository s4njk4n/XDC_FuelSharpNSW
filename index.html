      Things to know

      Reading the map

      Pins show the price in cents per litre, coloured against everything on screen: cheapest third, middle, dearest third. The cheapest get a price label and the rest are dots, so zoom in for more labels. Tap any pin for all its prices and directions.

      Where the prices come from

      Stations must report every price change to the NSW Government’s FuelCheck system, and FuelSharp collects the full list every hour. The status at the top shows how fresh it is, and each station shows when its price last changed. Always confirm at the pump.

      AUDD, XDC and USDC

      AUDD is the Australian Digital Dollar, a stablecoin backed 1:1 by Australian dollars and available on XDC Network, so AUDD prices match the pump. XDC and USDC prices are converted at the current market rate shown under Settings.

      Your data and privacy

      Your discounts and settings are saved only in this browser. Your location is used on your device to centre the map and is never sent to XDC Outpost. Like the rest of xdcoutpost.xyz, this page counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies, plus anonymous counts of which features get used, such as the fuel type picked or a tap on “Find fuel near me”. Those counts never include your location, discounts or prices. Map images come from OpenStreetMap, exchange rates from CoinGecko and directions from Google Maps, so, as with any site that uses them, those services receive your browser’s requests, including the map area you view.

      Use it like an app

      On Android, tap Install app. On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens straight to the prices and still shows the last ones it loaded when you’re offline.

      What’s covered

      Every NSW station reporting to FuelCheck, for U91, E10, P95, P98, Diesel, Premium Diesel, LPG and E85. Stations that don’t sell the fuel you pick are left off, rather than shown with a guessed price.
